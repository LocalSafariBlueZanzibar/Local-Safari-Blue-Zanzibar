About Us & Our Local Story

Welcome to Authentic Dhow Expeditions — a registered, 100% locally-owned sea excursion operator based in Fumba Village, sailing the protected waters of the Menai Bay Conservation Area since 2010.

14+ YearsLocal experience
100% NativeZanzibari captains
Eco-FriendlyMarine tourism

Why sail with us:

  • Business Name registered — Under the BPRA Zanzibar.
  • Pristine reefs & sandbanks — untouched coral gardens and quiet turquoise shallows.
  • Fresh seafood feast — line-caught BBQ with lobster, jumbo prawns, fish and calamari.
  • Direct & fair pricing — booking with us supports the Fumba community.

✨ Book your dhow safari with true local experts and experience the real heart of Zanzibar.

What is included in the trip?

Seafood BBQ lunch (fish, lobster, calamari), fresh tropical fruits, soft drinks, snorkeling gear (mask & fins), life jackets, and experienced local guides.

What time does the tour start and end?

Starts around 8:30–9:00 AM, finishes between 4:00–4:30 PM.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, sunscreen, hat, towel, water shoes, waterproof bag, and camera.

Do you offer hotel pickup and transfer?

Yes — private pickup from anywhere in Zanzibar (Stone Town, Nungwi, Kendwa, Paje, Jambiani) for an extra fee.

Are vegetarian or special diet meals available?

Yes. Vegetarian, vegan and chicken options on request. Please notify us in advance.

Is the tour suitable for children and non-swimmers?

Absolutely. Life jackets for all ages and crew assistance during snorkeling and sandbank stops.

How do I book and pay?

Book via the online booking form or on WhatsApp. Pay on arrival in cash (USD, EUR, TZS) or by mobile money.

What is the difference between a large corporate tour operator and Authentic Dhow Expeditions?

This is an honest, important question many guests ask. Both options will show you the same beautiful reefs and sandbanks of Zanzibar — but the experience itself is completely different:

Large corporate operators

  • Operate on a massive scale, grouping dozens of strangers onto large commercial boats.
  • Follow rigid, factory-like timetables with little flexibility.
  • You are often treated as a ticket number rather than an individual guest.
  • Higher prices due to agent and middleman commissions.

Authentic Dhow Expeditions (us)

  • Run directly by local captains born and raised on these coasts — small-group, personal adventures.
  • Flexible pacing focused entirely on your comfort and joy.
  • Genuine Swahili hospitality, local stories, and warm atmosphere that big companies cannot replicate.
  • Direct & fair pricing — your booking uplifts local Fumba families.

✨ Honest verdict: If you enjoy a massive commercial crowd with a strict corporate schedule, a large operator is fine. But if you want flexibility, small groups, better value, and a soulful connection with the true heart of Zanzibar — choosing Authentic Dhow Expeditions is a decision you will never regret.

Terms of Service

Last updated: January 2026

Booking & Payment: A booking request submitted through this website does not constitute a confirmed reservation until our team replies via WhatsApp or email. Payment is collected on the day of the tour (cash or mobile money). No prepayment is required.
